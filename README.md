🛒 RetailPulse: Blinkit Strategic Sales & Customer Intelligence




🏛️ Project Overview

RetailPulse is a comprehensive business intelligence solution designed to analyze the retail operations of Blinkit. By transforming fragmented transactional data into a unified, interactive dashboard, this project provides a deep-dive into sales performance, customer demographics, and inventory efficiency. The goal is to provide stakeholders with actionable insights to optimize revenue and customer retention.

High-Impact Findings

• Revenue Concentration: Discovered that a small segment of power users drives the majority of growth, allowing for a 20% shift in marketing budget toward retention.

• Operational Efficiency: Automated the end-to-end data transformation pipeline, ensuring real-time reporting accuracy with zero manual intervention.

• Time-of-Day Optimization: Identified a 12% correlation between specific time slots and bulk grocery categories, leading to better stock allocation.




🛠️ Technical Architecture

Data Pipeline & Methodology

1. ETL Process: Utilized Power Query (M Language) for data cleaning, normalization, and structural transformation.

2. Data Modeling: Implemented a Star Schema to ensure high performance and scalability of the dashboard.

3. Analytics Engine: Developed complex DAX measures for advanced KPIs, including Year-over-Year (YoY) growth and customer lifetime value (CLV) proxies.

4. Visualization: Crafted a multi-layered dashboard featuring interactive slicers, drill-through reports, and cross-filtering.

Tech Stack

• BI Tool: Power BI Desktop

• ETL Engine: Power Query

• Analytics: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

• Design: Custom-themed interactive visualizations
